RMSSD, SDNN and HR are medians of the rolling 60 s values, skipping each block's first minute. Breathing rate, swing and the HR low/high come from the block's own beats — the rolling medians average the swing away. Suspect beats are excluded from all of it. A block under 90 s is not measured. Breathing rate shows “—” unless two independent methods agree — a spectral peak and a count of the wave's own turning points. Guided breathing is measured to within 0.04/min; free breathing often cannot be measured at all, and a dash says so.
